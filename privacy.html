Solo HQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Solo HQ is a personal tool used by one person to manage and review their own short-form videos. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, Solo HQ reads the owner's own YouTube channel statistics and video analytics (for example views, watch time and subscriber counts) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. It uses read-only access and never posts, edits or deletes anything on YouTube.

How the data is used and stored

The data is shown only to the owner, in a dashboard running on the owner's own computer, and stored only there. It is not sold, shared with, or transferred to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Solo HQ's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
