Solo HQ Terms of Service

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Solo HQ is a personal tool used only by its owner to review their own social media accounts. It is not offered, sold or available to anyone else.

It reads the owner's own account statistics, with the owner's permission, and stores them only on the owner's computer. It does not post through these read-only connections. Access can be removed at any time from each platform's account settings.

See also the privacy policy.
